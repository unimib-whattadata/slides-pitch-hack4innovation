# Pitch Speech (Approx. 4 Minutes)

## Slide 1: Introduction (20 seconds)

"Good evening everyone

My name is Andrea, and today we will present our **AI Pipeline** to improve the hairing process.

## Slide 2: The Problem (45 seconds)

_Slide 2: CV screening is broken_

In fact Small and big companies have the same big problem: **hiring is slow.**
Recruiters lose hours every week on repetitive and sabjektive tasks.

Also, when people are tired, they make mistakes. Decisions are often based on 'feelings' instead of facts.
And the worst part? Most companies have **no real data** on their hiring. They don't know why a candidate was good or bad. It’s like driving a car with your eyes closed."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (45 seconds)

_Slide 3: Automated end-to-end pipeline_

"Our solution is an **automated pipeline**. It works like a 24/7 assistant.

1. First, it automatically checks your email for new CVs.
2. Then, it saves the files in a safe place, like Google Drive.
3. Finally, the smartest part: our AI reads the CV, understands the skills, and gives a score based on what you are looking for.
   It works day and night, it never gets tired, and it finishes the job in just a few minutes, with zero extra work from you."

---

## Slide 4: Real Results (45 seconds)

_Slide 4: Dashboard & Report_

"But what do you actually see? You get two simple things.
First, a **Digital Dashboard**. It’s a website where you can see all your candidates in one place. You can filter them, search for skills, and see charts about your progress.
Second, you get a **Simple Spreadsheet**. All the info—name, email, phone number, and LinkedIn—is automatically written into a Google Sheet for you.
No more copy-pasting. Everything is organized and ready to use immediately."

---

## Slide 5: The Value (30 seconds)

_Slide 5: The value we create_

"Why is this better?
Because it makes you **10 times faster**. Instead of waiting days, you have information in minutes.
It's also **fair**: the AI uses the same rules for everyone, so it's more balanced.
And finally, it’s **cheap**. We built this with simple tools, so it doesn't cost much to run. It doesn't matter if you have 10 candidates or 1,000—it just works."

## Slide 6: The Business Model (30 seconds)

_Slide 6: Strategy_

It’s perfect for any size of company.
It’s a 'ready-to-go' system: you connect your email, and it starts working. No complicated setup needed."
